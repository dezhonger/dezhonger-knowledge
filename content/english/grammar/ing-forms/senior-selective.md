### 进阶形式

`having done` 表示主动动作明显早于谓语：Having checked the data, she wrote the report。`being done` 表示正在承受动作；`having been done` 表示被动动作已经完成：Having been warned of the storm, the hikers changed their route。

并非所有先发生动作都必须用完成式；若先后关系由连词或常识已清楚，普通 doing 也可能自然。完成式用于需要特别强调时间先后的场景。

### 小练习

1. 填空：___ (finish) the survey, the team began to analyze the results.
2. 填空：The issue ___ (discuss) now has attracted attention.
3. 填空：___ (give) enough time, the experiment produced reliable results.

### 参考答案

1. `Having finished`。
2. `being discussed`。
3. `Having been given`。
