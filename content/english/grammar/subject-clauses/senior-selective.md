### 高中学习重点

常见形式主语结构包括 It is said/reported that...、It is important that...、It seems that... 和 It is a pity that...。部分表示建议、必要或命令的结构中，that 从句可用 `(should) + 动词原形`：It is essential that every member (should) participate。

what、whatever、whoever 等融合关系词既承担连接功能又在从句中作成分：Whatever happens will not change our decision。

### 小练习

1. 用 it 改写：That everyone understood the rule was important.
2. 填空：___ caused the failure is still being investigated.
3. 填空：It is necessary that the data ___ (check) again.

### 参考答案

1. It was important that everyone understood the rule。
2. `What`。
3. `(should) be checked`。
