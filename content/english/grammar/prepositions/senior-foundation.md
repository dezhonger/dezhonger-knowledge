### 高中学习重点

高中长句中，介词短语可能作定语、状语或补语。`the changes in climate` 修饰 changes；`with great care` 说明方式；`be aware of the risk` 是形容词补语。分析时先找到介词宾语，再判断整个短语修饰谁。

介词提前的定语从句较正式：the method **by which** the data were collected。介词选择由原搭配和意义决定，不能只看先行词。

### 小练习

1. 填空：The success of the plan depends ___ public support.
2. 改写：This is the tool which we measured the distance with.（介词提前）
3. 选择：**Despite / Despite of** the rain, the match continued.

### 参考答案

1. `on`。
2. `This is the tool with which we measured the distance.`
3. `Despite`。
