### 语篇中的使用

报道动词常用两类被动：It is believed that the plan will work；The plan is believed to work。若报道的动作早于谓语时间，可用完成不定式：The scientist is believed to have discovered the method earlier。

非谓语也有被动：to be invited、being discussed、having been completed。选择时同时判断逻辑主语承受动作以及动作与主句的时间关系。正式写作不必为了显得客观而滥用被动；若施事重要且主动表达更清楚，应保留主动句。

### 小练习

1. 改写：People say that the painting is over 500 years old.（以 The painting 开头）
2. 填空：She was pleased ___ (invite) to the conference.
3. 填空：___ (complete) ahead of schedule, the project received praise.

### 参考答案

1. The painting is said to be over 500 years old。
2. `to be invited`。
3. `Having been completed`。
