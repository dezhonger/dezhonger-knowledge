### 高中学习重点

构造被动时先保留主动句的时态，再把主要动词改为过去分词：They check the equipment → The equipment **is checked**；They checked it → It **was checked**。情态结构为 `情态动词 + be + done`，否定词放在第一个助动词后。

双宾语动词常有两种被动：They gave us new instructions → We were given new instructions / New instructions were given to us。

### 小练习

1. 改为被动：People use this method worldwide.
2. 填空：The application must ___ (submit) before Friday.
3. 改错：A new library will built next year.

### 参考答案

1. This method is used worldwide。
2. `be submitted`。
3. 改为 `will be built`。
