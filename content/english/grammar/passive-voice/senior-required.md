### 进阶形式

进行时被动为 `be being done`：The system is being updated。完成时被动为 `have been done`：The results have been published。多个助动词出现时，最后保留 `been/being + 过去分词`，不要遗漏 be 的形式。

短语动词变被动时要保持整体：People have looked into the problem → The problem has been looked into。含宾补的结构要根据动词决定补语形式：They made him apologize → He was made **to apologize**。

### 小练习

1. 填空：The old stadium ___ (replace) by a new one at present.
2. 改为被动：They have put off the meeting.
3. 改为被动：The teacher made the students rewrite the report.

### 参考答案

1. `is being replaced`。
2. The meeting has been put off。
3. The students were made to rewrite the report。
