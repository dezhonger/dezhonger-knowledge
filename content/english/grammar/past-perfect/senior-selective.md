### 高中学习重点

过去完成时常出现在 `It was the first time that...`、`Hardly/Scarcely had...when...`、`No sooner had...than...` 等结构中。后两类把 hardly/no sooner 置于句首时，主句需要部分倒装。

表示原本希望、打算却未实现，可用 had hoped/intended/planned to do：We had hoped to finish earlier, but the test took longer than expected。

### 小练习

1. 填空：It was the first time that she ___ (speak) in public.
2. 补全：No sooner ___ we arrived than the meeting began.
3. 选择：By 2020, the team **completed / had completed** three trials.

### 参考答案

1. `had spoken`。
2. `had`。
3. `had completed`，2020 是过去参照点。
