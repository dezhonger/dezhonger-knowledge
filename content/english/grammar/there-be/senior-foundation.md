### 高中学习重点

there be 可以与完成、情态和非谓语结构结合：There has been a sharp increase；There seems to be no simple answer；We expect there to be changes。正式写作中还可使用 exist、remain、appear 等存在类动词：There remains one concern。

并列名词后的就近一致在考试中常见：There is a desk and two chairs in the room；调整顺序后为 There are two chairs and a desk。

### 小练习

1. 填空：There ___ (be) several changes since last year.
2. 改错：There will have more opportunities in the future.
3. 填空：There ___ a pen and three notebooks on the table.

### 参考答案

1. `have been`。
2. `will have` 改为 `will be`。
3. `is`，按最靠近的 a pen 保持一致。
