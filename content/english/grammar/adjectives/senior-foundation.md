### 高中学习重点

形容词作补语时补充主语或宾语状态：The door remained open；They painted the door blue。某些形容词主要作表语，如 afraid、alive；某些前置与后置意义不同，如 the people present 指“在场的人”。

比较结构要保持平行：The climate of Kunming is milder than **that of** Beijing，不能直接把 climate 与 Beijing 比较。`the + 比较级..., the + 比较级...` 表示两个变化相关：The more carefully you plan, the less time you waste。

### 小练习

1. 改错：The weather here is warmer than Beijing.
2. 选择：The instructions were **confused / confusing**, so several students felt **confused / confusing**.
3. 补全：The more evidence we collect, ___ our conclusion becomes.（strong）

### 参考答案

1. 改为 `warmer than that in Beijing`。
2. `confusing; confused`。
3. `the stronger`。
