### 高中学习重点

百分比、分数和 `most/some/all of` 的谓语取决于 of 后名词：Two thirds of the land **is** covered；Two thirds of the students **are** present。时间、距离、金钱作为一个总量时常用单数：Ten kilometers is a long walk。

主语后跟 together with、as well as、including 等结构时，谓语仍与前面的主语一致：The teacher, together with the students, is visiting the museum。

### 小练习

1. 填空：Each of the proposals ___ (need) further discussion.
2. 填空：The number of visitors ___ rising, while a number of rooms ___ still available.
3. 填空：Neither the coach nor the players ___ satisfied.

### 参考答案

1. `needs`。
2. `is; are`。
3. `are`，与邻近的 players 一致。
