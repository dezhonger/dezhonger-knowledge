### 高中学习重点

混合虚拟条件句的从句和主句指向不同时间：If I had taken the advice then, I would be better prepared now。含蓄条件可由 without、but for、otherwise 等表达：Without your help, we could not have finished。

`would rather + 从句` 表现在或将来的愿望时从句常用一般过去时，表过去遗憾时用过去完成时：I'd rather you came tomorrow；I'd rather you had told me earlier。

### 小练习

1. 填空：If I ___ (know) about the change yesterday, I would have adjusted the plan.
2. 填空：The doctor recommended that he ___ (rest) for a week.
3. 改写：Without your reminder, I would have forgotten.（使用 if）

### 参考答案

1. `had known`。
2. `(should) rest`。
3. If you had not reminded me, I would have forgotten。
