### 高中学习重点

强调人时可用 who 或 that，强调其他成分通常用 that。强调句中的 be 根据原句时间用 is/was，但被强调部分的人称和数不决定 be：It is my parents who encourage me。

`not...until` 的强调结构为 `It was not until ... that ...`，that 后恢复陈述语序：It was not until midnight that the work was finished。与 Not until 置首引起的倒装不要混淆。

### 小练习

1. 强调地点：We first met in the library.
2. 改错：It was Tom that did solved the problem.
3. 用强调句改写：We did not understand the rule until the teacher explained it again.

### 参考答案

1. It was in the library that we first met。
2. 去掉 did，写 It was Tom that/who solved the problem。
3. It was not until the teacher explained it again that we understood the rule。
