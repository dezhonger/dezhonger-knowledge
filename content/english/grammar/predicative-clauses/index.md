## 核心规则

表语从句位于 be、seem、look 等系动词之后，对主语进行解释或说明。常用连接词包括 that、whether、what、who、why、how、where、when、because 和 as if/as though。

## 结构与例句

The fact is that we need more evidence。The question is whether the change is practical。This is what we discovered。That is why the meeting was delayed。It looks as if it is going to rain。

## 使用场景

表语从句特别适合组织“问题—答案”“原因—结果”和“事实—内容”。连接词要准确对应逻辑：The reason is that...；This is because...；This is why...。

## 常见错误

**错误：** The reason is because the road was closed. → 标准表达常写 The reason is that the road was closed。

**错误：** The question is if we should continue. → 正式表语从句通常使用 whether。

## 易混知识点

because 引出原因，why 引出结果：The road was closed. That is why we were late；We were late. That is because the road was closed。

## 总结

先识别主语需要补充的是内容、选择、原因还是结果，再选择对应连接词。
