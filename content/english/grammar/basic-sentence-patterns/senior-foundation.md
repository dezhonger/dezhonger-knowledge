### 高中学习重点

高中阶段不仅要记五种基本句型，还要识别必须出现的状语。`The meeting lasted` 语法上勉强完整但信息常不足，`The meeting lasted two hours` 中 two hours 是动词语义要求的重要补充。`put` 则通常同时需要宾语和位置：Put the files on the desk。

双宾语结构改写时介词由动词决定：give/send/show something to somebody；buy/make something for somebody。感官和使役动词后的宾补形式还会影响语义。

### 小练习

1. 判断句型：The audience found the lecture inspiring.
2. 改错：He suggested me to take a break.
3. 改写：She bought her brother a dictionary.（使用介词）

### 参考答案

1. SVOC。
2. 改为 `He suggested that I take a break` 或 `He suggested taking a break`。
3. She bought a dictionary for her brother。
