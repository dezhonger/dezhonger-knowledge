### 基本功能

过去分词短语作后置定语可还原为被动定语从句：the samples collected yesterday = the samples that were collected yesterday。`have/get + 宾语 + done` 可表示让别人做、使某事完成或遭遇某事：I had my bike repaired；He had his wallet stolen。

感官动词后的过去分词强调宾语承受动作或处于结果状态：I heard my name called。

### 小练习

1. 改写：the books that were donated by local families（使用分词）
2. 填空：She had her computer ___ (repair) yesterday.
3. 填空：I noticed the door ___ (leave) open.

### 参考答案

1. `the books donated by local families`。
2. `repaired`。
3. `left`。
