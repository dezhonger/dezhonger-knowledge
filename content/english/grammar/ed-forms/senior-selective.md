### 进阶功能

过去分词作状语可表示时间、原因、条件、让步或伴随：Asked about the result, she smiled；Given more time, we could improve the design。必要时可保留 when、if、although 等连词，使关系更明确。

若分词的逻辑主语不同于主句主语，需要独立主格：The work finished, everyone went home。部分固定表达如 given、considering 已趋向介词或连词用法，要结合整体理解。

### 小练习

1. 改写：Because he was encouraged by his teacher, he tried again.（使用分词）
2. 填空：___ (see) from above, the fields form a pattern.
3. 补全独立结构：The meeting ___, the participants left the room.

### 参考答案

1. Encouraged by his teacher, he tried again。
2. `Seen`。
3. `over/finished`，构成名词 + 形容词或过去分词的独立结构。
