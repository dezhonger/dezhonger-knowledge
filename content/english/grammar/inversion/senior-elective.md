### 高中学习重点

hardly、rarely、seldom、little、nowhere、by no means、not until 等否定或近否定表达置于句首时，主句部分倒装。Not until the data was checked **did we publish** the result；从句本身不倒装。

so/neither/nor 可表示“也一样”：She can swim, and so can I；He didn't complain, neither did I。条件句可省略 if，将 had、were、should 提前：Had it not rained, we would have left earlier。

### 小练习

1. 改写：I had never heard such an explanation.（Never 置首）
2. 补全：Only after the test ___ we realize the mistake.
3. 用倒装改写：If I were you, I would wait.

### 参考答案

1. Never had I heard such an explanation。
2. `did`。
3. Were I you, I would wait。
