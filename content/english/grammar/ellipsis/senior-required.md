### 高中学习重点

状语从句可在主语与主句主语一致、谓语含 be 时省略“主语 + be”：Although (she was) tired, she kept working；If (it is) possible, reply today。固定结构 if necessary、when possible、once completed 也很常见。

不定式省略时常保留 to：You may leave if you want to。若 be 或 have 是被省动词的一部分，有时需保留：He isn't a doctor, but he wants to be。

### 小练习

1. 省略重复部分：When you are crossing the road, stay alert.
2. 补全：She hasn't finished the task, but she hopes to ___.
3. 判断能否省略关系词：The student who won the prize thanked her teacher.

### 参考答案

1. When crossing the road, stay alert。
2. 可只保留 `to`，后面的 finish the task 由语境恢复。
3. 不能，who 在从句中作主语。
