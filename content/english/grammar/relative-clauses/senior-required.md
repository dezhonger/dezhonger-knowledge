### 关系词的选择

先找先行词，再判断从句缺少什么成分。who、whom、which、that、whose 在从句中充当名词性成分；when、where、why 充当状语。地点名词并不自动搭配 where：the place **which we visited** 中 visited 缺宾语；the place **where we stayed** 中从句缺地点状语。

> **The device that measures air quality was designed by students.**\
> 测量空气质量的设备由学生设计。

关系词作宾语时常可省略：the book (that) I borrowed；作主语时不能省略。

### 小练习

1. 填空：The scientist ___ discovery changed medicine won the prize.
2. 选择：This is the village **where / which** we visited last year.
3. 改错：The girl who she answered the question is my sister.

### 参考答案

1. `whose`，在从句中表示 discovery 的所属关系。
2. `which`，visited 后缺宾语。
3. 删除 she，who 已在从句中作主语。
