### 高中学习重点

that 在从句中不充当成分；what 既引导从句又在从句中作主语、宾语或表语。比较 I know **that** he needs（结构不完整，needs 后缺宾语）与 I know **what** he needs（what 作 needs 的宾语）。

否定转移常见于 think、believe、suppose 等：I don't think he is right 比 I think he isn't right 更自然。形式宾语 it 可代替较长从句：We find it important that every voice is heard。

### 小练习

1. 填空：Could you tell me where the nearest bank ___ (be)?
2. 选择：It depends on **if / whether** the weather improves.
3. 填空：I don't know ___ caused the delay.

### 参考答案

1. `is`。
2. `whether`。
3. `what`，在从句中作主语。
