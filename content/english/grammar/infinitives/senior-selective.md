### 进阶形式

`to be doing` 强调与谓语同时进行：He seems to be sleeping。`to have done` 表示动作早于谓语：She appears to have solved the problem。被动形式为 `to be done`，完成被动为 `to have been done`。

不定式有自己的逻辑主语时，常用 `for + 名词/代词 + to do`：It is necessary for us to prepare。描述人的品质时常用 `of`：It was kind of you to help。

### 小练习

1. 填空：The bridge is expected ___ (complete) next year.
2. 填空：He seems ___ (forget) our earlier conversation.
3. 选择：It is difficult **for / of** beginners to master the pattern.

### 参考答案

1. `to be completed`。
2. `to have forgotten`。
3. `for`，difficult 描述任务对 beginners 的难度。
