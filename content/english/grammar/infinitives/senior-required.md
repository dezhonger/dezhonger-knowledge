### 基本功能

不定式作目的状语时可用 in order to 或 so as to 强调；否定形式是 not to do。作后置定语时，它与所修饰名词可能是动宾、主谓或同位关系：a book to read；the first person to arrive；a plan to protect wildlife。

感官动词和使役动词后的宾补在主动句中可省略 to：I saw him cross the road；She made me wait。变为被动后，make 的 to 恢复：I was made to wait。

### 小练习

1. 填空：She got up early ___ (avoid) the traffic.
2. 改错：The teacher made us to rewrite the paragraph.
3. 判断功能：He is the first student to finish the task.

### 参考答案

1. `to avoid`。
2. 删除 `to`。
3. 不定式作后置定语，修饰 student。
