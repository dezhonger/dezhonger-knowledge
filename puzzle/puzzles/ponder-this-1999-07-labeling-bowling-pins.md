## IBM Ponder This #015 · July 1999

Ten bowling pins are arranged in a triangle and labelled with the distinct integers $0,1,\ldots,9$. Whenever two pins are side by side, the sum of their labels, reduced modulo $10$, equals the label of the pin in front of and between them.

> How are the pins labelled?
