## IBM Ponder This #004 · August 1998

Take a triangle $ABC$. Choose points

$$
D\in AB,\qquad E\in BC,\qquad F\in CA.
$$

Suppose the inner triangle $DEF$ is equilateral and that

$$
AD=BE=CF.
$$

> Prove that $ABC$ is also equilateral.
