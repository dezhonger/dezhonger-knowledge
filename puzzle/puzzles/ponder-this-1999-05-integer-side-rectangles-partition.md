## IBM Ponder This #013 · May 1999

A large rectangle is partitioned into smaller rectangles whose sides are parallel to those of the large rectangle. Every small rectangle has at least one side with integer length; for different rectangles, this may be a horizontal or a vertical side.

> Prove that the large rectangle also has at least one side with integer length.
