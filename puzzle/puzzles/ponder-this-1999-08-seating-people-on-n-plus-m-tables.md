## IBM Ponder This #016 · August 1999

At every meal, $M+N$ people are seated at two separate tables: one with $M$ seats and one with $N$ seats. To “sit with” someone means to sit at the same table, not necessarily next to them.

> How many meals are needed so that every person sits with every other person at least once?

At a given meal, everyone occupies one seat throughout the meal and the tables do not overlap.
