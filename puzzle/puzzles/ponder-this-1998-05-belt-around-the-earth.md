## IBM Ponder This #001 · May 1998

Imagine a belt fitted tightly around the Earth's equator. Its length is then increased by exactly **6 metres**.

Lift one point of the belt upward until the belt is taut again. The two sides of the lifted belt are straight, while the remaining part still follows the Earth.

> How high above the surface is the lifted point?
